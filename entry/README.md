# The poem about permission

A make.fun entry for the [the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235) bounty: a short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given.

## The poem

> I asked, and the door learned my name.
> Not a knock, but a key —
> a small yes, practiced daily,
> until the room rearranged itself
> around the sound of being allowed.
> Ask. The world is listening
> for the part of you that dares
> to be given.
